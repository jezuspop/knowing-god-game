# Knowing God: host notes and sources

This game draws on the seven public Knowing God lessons and their related Scripture passages. The questions were checked against relevant pages of all seven public lesson PDFs. The review did not cover every page or separately inspect the Keynotes.

- [Series overview](https://www.gpcc.org/kgs)
- Lesson 01: [Knowing God](https://www.gpcc.org/knowing-god) — Knowing God & His Word, 100.
- Lesson 02: [Knowing God’s Word](https://www.gpcc.org/knowing-gods-word) — Knowing God & His Word, 200–500.
- Lesson 03: [Knowing God’s Calling](https://www.gpcc.org/knowing-gods-calling) — The Call to Follow and optional finale.
- Lesson 04: [Knowing God’s Holiness](https://www.gpcc.org/knowing-gods-holiness) — From Darkness to Light.
- Lesson 05: [Knowing God’s Plan](https://www.gpcc.org/knowing-gods-plan) — God’s Plan & Pentecost.
- Lesson 06: [Knowing God’s Son](https://www.gpcc.org/knowing-gods-son) — Jesus & His Family, 300.
- Lesson 07: [Knowing God’s Family](https://www.gpcc.org/knowing-gods-family) — Jesus & His Family, 100, 200, 400, and 500.

Invite paraphrases and use the answers as a guide. Jeopardy-style question wording is optional; the host can award partial credit and choose whether or not to deduct points. The finale can be played for fun or any amount the host chooses.
